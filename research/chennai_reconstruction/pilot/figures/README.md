# Pilot figures

These figures show **metadata footprints only**. No Sentinel-1 VV/VH backscatter, valid-observation masks, change layers or common-coverage rasters were produced because the approved products could not be downloaded and no SNAP/pyroSAR toolchain is installed.

## Produced

- `footprints_2015-12-06.png` — metadata footprint and pilot bbox.
- `footprints_2021-11-04.png` — north/south frame footprints and bbox.
- `footprints_2021-11-16.png` — north/south frame footprints and bbox.
- `coverage_summary.png` — footprint coverage fractions (not valid pixels).

## Not produced (blocked)

- VV backscatter and VH backscatter.
- Valid-observation mask and no-data/quality mask.
- 2021 VV change and VH change.
- Common valid-observation coverage.
- Urban layover/shadow or border-artifact maps.

Do not interpret the footprint outlines as a flood boundary.
